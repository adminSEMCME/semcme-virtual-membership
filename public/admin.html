Manage the member library here. Changes appear for members when they reload the page. Assign only confirmed category connections; leave unfinished reviews unchecked.

How to manage materials, categories, and series

Add or edit a material

On the Materials tab, search for an existing title and select it, or choose Add material. Enter the title, full link, and material type. Add presenter names and a date when known. Keep Play YouTube videos within the site checked for recordings.

Choose where it appears

Check every confirmed competency and survey area. A material can appear in several categories. Removing a check removes that connection after saving. Category assignments from the approved November 2026 mapping document have already been added.

Mark each group's review complete only when its mapping decision is finished. Leave unknown or to-be-determined groups unchecked. Materials with unfinished reviews remain in All Materials and any confirmed categories.

Additional Topics: complete both reviews and leave all categories unchecked only when it has been confirmed that neither group applies. A blank mapping alone does not mean Additional Topics.

Group related sessions

Use Series & sessions to create or rename a series. Then edit each material, choose that series, and enter its session order (1, 2, 3, and so on). Save each material separately. Each session keeps its own category connections. Deleting a series keeps its materials.

Manage categories

Use Categories to add, rename, or hide a competency or survey area. Hiding a category keeps its assignments but removes the category from member browsing. A category must have no assigned materials before it can be deleted.

Save, hide, or remove

Click Save and confirm the change. Save the current form before editing another tab. To temporarily hide a material, uncheck Include in the updated member library and save. Delete material permanently removes it and its category connections from this library. The original imported source entry is kept separately.

Use Refresh library to load saved changes. The Needs review filter helps find unfinished mappings. Internal review notes and original-location information are visible only to administrators.
